# 🎯 Project Overview

## 1. Goals
The **Cherry x K COFFEE** website is a digital exhibition and charity landing page. It showcases the artworks of the 13-year-old artist Cherry, with the goal of raising funds for children's hospitals (Nhi Đồng 1 & 2) via the K COFFEE & Cherry Charity Fund.

## 2. Sitemap & Structure
- **Hero:** Main banner, introductory text.
- **Project Info:** Key metrics (80% donation, 80+ artworks).
- **Author:** Information about Cherry.
- **Gallery:** Horizontal scroll gallery of artworks.
- **History/Impact:** Story of the charity fund and stats.
- **News:** Press and media slider.
- **Testimonials:** Quotes from experts and partners.
- **Schedule:** Exhibition timeline and locations.
- **Merchandise:** Products featuring Cherry's art.
- **Contact:** Purchase contact info and message form.

## 3. Core Requirements
- Must remain a static single-page application (SPA style).
- Fast loading times, minimal dependencies.
- Highly immersive and emotional design (Vibe Coding).
